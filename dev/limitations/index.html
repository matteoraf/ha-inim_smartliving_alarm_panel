    Known limitations¶

    Known limitations of this version, and what you can do about them:

    • The integration polls the panel (the areas every 2 seconds by default, see Polling), so an alarm that starts and ends between two reads never shows as Triggered on the alarm panel entity or on the Area N alarm sensor. The alarm memory sensors of the area and the zone stay on afterwards and show that it happened, until the memory is reset (see Resetting the alarm memory).

    • While Home Assistant reads the panel at the Polling intervals, it keeps its connection to the board open. The SmartLAN/SI board accepts one local client at a time, so other apps on your network (such as the Inim app or the installer software) cannot connect to it meanwhile. Turn on slow polling mode to let them connect between Home Assistant's reads, which then come every 30 seconds. Remote access through Inim Cloud is not affected.

    • The event log names every event the panel logs, and who and what it refers to. Emergency keys and programmable events are shown by number (Emergency key 1, Programmable event 2): their names are not read from the panel.

    • Panels on firmware 5.0x or of another model that were set up with an earlier version keep running with the 1050's firmware 6 layout, so their names, zones, scenarios and event log may be wrong and their areas' delays are not shown. A repair notice says so. New setups of such panels are refused.

    • Dimmer levels are read from the panel on firmware 6 only: firmware 5.1 does not report them. On firmware 5.1 a dimmer's brightness is the last one set from Home Assistant, so a level changed at a keypad is not shown.

    • Keypad thermostats: the daily and weekly programs can only be changed at the keypad. The target shown for a program follows Home Assistant's clock, while the panel follows its own. Firmware 5.1 does not report whether a thermostat is heating or cooling right now, and the thermostats have been checked on a 1050 with firmware 6.07 over SmartLAN/SI only, not on firmware 5.1 or with a SmartLAN/G board. A room temperature below 0 °C is shown as unknown.

    • Analogue outputs are not offered. A terminal output on one of the panel's first three terminal positions cannot be switched remotely: the panel's output command cannot address those positions.

    • Support for firmware 5.1 is experimental and limited: it has not been tested on a real panel on firmware 5.1; the panel's state flags (such as programming mode) and the results of commands are assumed to work as on firmware 6; the free events that only firmware 5.1 has are shown as Free event 1 to Free event 14; and firmware 5.1 has no video sensors.
